# Trenches Kombat: art prompts for the 15 new fighters

Two images per fighter, in this order:

1. **The card.** Upload **their profile picture** and **one existing Trenches Kombat card** (any of
   the first 31, for the frame style), then paste the CARD prompt. Save it as `<id>-card.png`.
2. **The pose sheet.** Start a new chat, upload **the card you just made**, then paste the POSE prompt.
   Save it as `<id>.png`.

Send me both. The card fixes the character's look, so the poses match it.

If the tool refuses because the picture is a real person or a famous character, remove the
profile picture and use the written description only (it's already in the prompt).

## 1. Xeverade  (`xeverade`)

**CARD prompt:** save as `xeverade-card.png`

```
Create a vertical trading card (2:3 portrait) for a 2D anime fighting game called Trenches Kombat.
Use the first uploaded image as the character reference and the second uploaded image as the exact card style (frame, nameplate, art style).
Character: an armoured sorcerer: polished steel face mask with rivets, deep green hooded cloak, steel chest armour with a round emblem, steel gauntlets.
Turn them into a cool anime fighter: dramatic cel-shaded art, the character from head to knees in a confident fighting stance, glowing green energy and light particles around them.
Background: glowing green magic, a dark stone throne room with red banners.
Ornate gold card frame with a dark inner border, and a gold nameplate banner at the bottom with the name "XEVERADE" in elegant serif capital letters and a small gold crown above it.
No other text, no logos, no watermark.
```

**POSE prompt:** save as `xeverade.png`

```
Using the uploaded card as the character reference, make ONE 2D fighting game sprite sheet of the SAME character: an armoured sorcerer: polished steel face mask with rivets, deep green hooded cloak, steel chest armour with a round emblem, steel gauntlets.

Draw 20 separate full-body poses in a grid of 5 columns and 4 rows, every pose facing RIGHT (side view), all the same size, evenly spaced, none touching or overlapping:
Row 1: fighting stance, fists up in guard | walking forward, fists up | crouching low in guard | jumping, knees tucked up | blocking with both forearms in front of the face
Row 2: quick jab, front arm fully extended | power punch, rear arm fully extended | high side kick, front leg fully extended at chest height | low leg sweep, crouched with one leg extended along the floor | uppercut, fist driving straight up
Row 3: overhead strike, both fists swinging down from above the head | both palms pushed forward releasing an energy blast | one arm raised high summoning power | dashing forward, body leaning far forward | grabbing forward with both hands
Row 4: getting hit, head snapped back | dazed and wobbling, arms hanging loose | knocked down, lying flat on the back | getting up, one knee down, hand on the floor | victory pose, one fist raised high

Rules:
- Exact same face, hair, outfit, colours and accessories as the card in all 20 poses. Same anime cel-shaded style.
- Every pose is different and shows the whole body, head to toe, nothing cropped.
- Realistic human proportions and balance, like Mortal Kombat / Street Fighter sprites.
- Plain solid flat white background (#FFFFFF). No frames, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers, no shadows, no effects.
- Large, high-resolution landscape image.
```
