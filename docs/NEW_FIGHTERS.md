# Trenches Kombat: new fighters (batch 2)

15 new fighters. Each one has a style, three specials, a fatality and a win quote. Specials are
used with the SPECIAL button (stick centred / forward / down), or with the motion inputs on a
keyboard. They follow the same 7-second cooldown as everyone else.

When you send the art, label it with the name (or the id in brackets): one **card** and one
**20-pose sheet** per fighter. The pose prompt is in `docs/POSE_PROMPTS.md`.

---

## 1. Xeverade · @xeverade  (`xeverade`)
**Style:** Technical / Precision. A clean, cold executioner who wins with perfect spacing and punishes every mistake.
- **Ever Blade:** throws a fast crescent of light along the ground.
- **Clean Entry:** vanishes and reappears behind the opponent with a slash.
- **Exit Liquidity:** a counter stance. If hit, steps aside and knocks the attacker down.

**Fatality, Final Exit:** freezes the opponent inside a giant red candle, slices the candle in half, and it shatters into confetti.
**Win quote:** "Perfect entry. Perfect exit."

## 2. Skroot · @skrootimburg  (`skroot`)
**Style:** Scrappy brawler / Pressure. Hits fast, slides in low and never gives you room to breathe.
- **Skrrt Skrrt:** a low drift slide that sweeps the opponent off their feet.
- **Burnout:** spins on the spot and throws a cloud of tyre smoke forward.
- **Pull Up:** runs in and grabs, ending with a shoulder throw.

**Fatality, Donuts:** does donuts around the opponent until they disappear in tyre smoke, leaving skid marks that spell "GG".
**Win quote:** "Skrrt. You blinked."

## 3. Otaobayom · @Otaobayom  (`otaobayom`)

- **Talking Drum:** beats a drum that sends a shockwave along the floor.
- **Palace Guard:** royal armour that absorbs the next few hits.

**Fatality, Royal Decree:** a giant golden scroll unrolls reading "BANISHED" and rolls the opponent up inside it.
