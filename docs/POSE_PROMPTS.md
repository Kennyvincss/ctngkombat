# Trenches Kombat: pose prompts (1 image per fighter)

One prompt, one image, all 20 poses. Nothing to fill in.

## How to do it

1. Open a free image tool (Gemini, ChatGPT, Microsoft Designer / Bing Image Creator, Meta AI or Grok).
2. **Upload the fighter's card.**
3. Copy the prompt, paste it, send. Download the image.
4. Name it `<fighter-id>.png` (for example `king-sol.png`) and send it to me.
   I cut out every pose and wire it in.

**Two options:**
- **Quick option:** the universal prompt right below works for *every* fighter, because the uploaded card shows who it is.
- **More accurate option:** scroll to your fighter's section. Same prompt, with the outfit described in words too.

If the image comes out wrong (poses facing left, feet cut off, repeated poses, fewer than 20), reply in the same chat:
*"Redo it. All 20 poses different, all facing right, full body with feet visible, plain white background, 5 columns and 4 rows."*

---

# Universal prompt (any fighter)

```
Using the uploaded card as the character reference, make ONE 2D fighting game sprite sheet of the SAME character shown on the uploaded card.

Draw 20 separate full-body poses in a grid of 5 columns and 4 rows, every pose facing RIGHT (side view), all the same size, evenly spaced, none touching or overlapping:
Row 1: fighting stance, fists up in guard | walking forward, fists up | crouching low in guard | jumping, knees tucked up | blocking with both forearms in front of the face
Row 2: quick jab, front arm fully extended | power punch, rear arm fully extended | high side kick, front leg fully extended at chest height | low leg sweep, crouched with one leg extended along the floor | uppercut, fist driving straight up
Row 3: overhead strike, both fists swinging down from above the head | both palms pushed forward releasing an energy blast | one arm raised high summoning power | dashing forward, body leaning far forward | grabbing forward with both hands
Row 4: getting hit, head snapped back | dazed and wobbling, arms hanging loose | knocked down, lying flat on the back | getting up, one knee down, hand on the floor | victory pose, one fist raised high

Rules:
- Exact same face, hair, outfit, colours and accessories as the card in all 20 poses. Same anime cel-shaded style.
- Every pose is different and shows the whole body, head to toe, nothing cropped.
- Realistic human proportions and balance, like Mortal Kombat / Street Fighter sprites.
- Plain solid flat white background (#FFFFFF). No frames, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers, no shadows, no effects.
- Large, high-resolution landscape image.
```
