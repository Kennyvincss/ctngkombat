# Trenches Kombat: batch 5 sprite sheets (40 poses per fighter)

Two sheets of 20 poses per fighter. Same chat for both: upload **that fighter's card**, paste
SHEET A, save it as `<id>-a.png`; then paste SHEET B in the same chat, save it as `<id>-b.png`.
Sheet A is the original 20-pose layout; sheet B adds 20 more (second walk step, jump arcs,
air and crouch attacks, throws, taunts and big reactions).

## SHEET A

```
Using the uploaded card as the character reference, make ONE 2D fighting game sprite sheet of the SAME character from the card (same face, hair, outfit, colours and accessories). If the character is an animal, robot or mascot, keep it standing upright on two legs with arms and fists, exactly as on the card.

Draw 20 separate full-body poses in a grid of 5 columns and 4 rows, every pose facing RIGHT (side view), all the same size, evenly spaced, none touching or overlapping:
Row 1: fighting stance, fists up in guard | walking forward, fists up | crouching low in guard | jumping, knees tucked up | blocking with both forearms in front of the face
Row 2: quick jab, front arm fully extended | power punch, rear arm fully extended | high side kick, front leg fully extended at chest height | low leg sweep, crouched with one leg extended along the floor | uppercut, fist driving straight up
Row 3: overhead strike, both fists swinging down from above the head | both palms pushed forward releasing an energy blast | one arm raised high summoning power | dashing forward, body leaning far forward | grabbing forward with both hands
Row 4: getting hit, head snapped back | dazed and wobbling, arms hanging loose | knocked down, lying flat on the back | getting up, one knee down, hand on the floor | victory pose, one fist raised high

Rules:
- Exact same character as the card in all 20 poses. Same anime cel-shaded style.
- Every pose is different and shows the whole body, head to toe, nothing cropped.
- Realistic proportions and balance, like Mortal Kombat / Street Fighter sprites.
- Plain solid flat white background (#FFFFFF). No frames, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers, no shadows, no glow, no effects.
- Large, high-resolution landscape image.
```

## SHEET B

```
Now make a SECOND sprite sheet of the SAME character, with 20 NEW poses (none repeated from the first sheet). Same face, hair, outfit, colours, size and art style as the first sheet.

Draw 20 separate full-body poses in a grid of 5 columns and 4 rows, every pose facing RIGHT (side view), all the same size as before, evenly spaced, none touching or overlapping:
Row 1: fighting stance breathing out, shoulders slightly lower than the first stance | walking forward, the OTHER leg stepping forward | walking backward, leaning away, guard up | jumping upward, body stretched, arms rising | landing from a jump, knees bent, arms out for balance
Row 2: crouching punch, low jab with the front arm | flying kick in mid-air, one leg extended diagonally downward | spinning back kick, back turned, rear leg extended | knee strike, knee driven up to chest height | elbow strike, front elbow swung forward at head height
Row 3: charging power, crouched with both fists clenched at the hips | throwing something forward with one arm, overarm like a pitcher | flying forward fist-first through the air, body horizontal | slamming one fist into the ground, kneeling on one knee | throwing an opponent over the shoulder, both arms swinging back
Row 4: crouching block, forearms covering the head while crouched | hit in the stomach, doubled over forward | blown backward through the air, arms and legs flailing | taunt, one hand beckoning "come on" | entrance pose, arms crossed, chin up, confident

Rules:
- Exact same character as the card and the first sheet in all 20 poses. Same anime cel-shaded style.
- Every pose is different and shows the whole body, head to toe, nothing cropped.
- Realistic proportions and balance, like Mortal Kombat / Street Fighter sprites.
- Plain solid flat white background (#FFFFFF). No frames, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no numbers, no shadows, no glow, no effects.
- Large, high-resolution landscape image.
```
